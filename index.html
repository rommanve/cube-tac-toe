How to play
  1. Player 1 is X, Player 2 is O.
  2. On your turn, click any empty white sticker to place your mark.
  3. Then make exactly one quarter turn — press a move button, press its letter key (hold Shift for a prime turn), or drag across a face of the cube.
  4. Drag the background any time to spin the cube and inspect all six sides.
  5. Three of your marks in a row, column or diagonal on a single face wins — but it is only checked after the turn completes, so a careless move can undo your line or hand one to your opponent.
